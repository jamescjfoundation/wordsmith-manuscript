# Roadmap

What is planned for Wordsmith Manuscript is the
[`v1` milestone](https://github.com/jamescjfoundation/wordsmith-manuscript/milestone/1) of this
repository's issues. This file holds no list, so it cannot fall behind.

- The first public release is 0.9.0, a preview; 1.0.0 follows when the milestone is done
  (decision 0009).
- Ideas for after that are the
  [open issues with no milestone](https://github.com/jamescjfoundation/wordsmith-manuscript/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+no%3Amilestone).
- What Wordsmith Book has asked for is in `docs/book-handoff.md`, "Requests from Book", each
  with its issue.
- What the app waits for in open-source-core is in `docs/core-candidates.md`.
- How the app came to be is `docs/PLAN.md`, the finished build plan, and `docs/progress.md`,
  the log of every step.

To propose something, open an issue.
