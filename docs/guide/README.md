# Wordsmith Manuscript guide

Wordsmith Manuscript is for writing novels and other long work: a binder of parts, chapters and
scenes, index cards and an outline for planning, a story bible beside the text, targets,
snapshots, and exports for editors and for Wordsmith Book.

1. [Installing and updating](installing.md): the download for your computer, how to open it, and what the update check asks.
2. [Getting started](getting-started.md): a new project, the window, the command palette and your first words.
3. [The binder](binder.md): parts, chapters and scenes in one tree.
4. [Writing](writing.md): the editor, the whole book as one text, pages, revision passes and comments.
5. [Planning](planning.md): the corkboard, the outline, the inspector, filters, targets and the writing history.
6. [The story bible](bible.md): characters, places and plot threads beside the text.
7. [Importing](import.md): documents, sets of drafts, notes folders, outlines and books.
8. [Exporting](export.md): the standard manuscript, other formats, and the book package for Wordsmith Book.
9. [Files, saving and backups](files.md): the project folder, recovery, backups and other versions of the app.
10. [Keys](keys.md): the default key for every command.
11. [The command line and agents](command-line-and-agents.md): the app's work without its window, and its commands as tools for an agent.
